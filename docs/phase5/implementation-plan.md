# PPL-HR Phase 5 Implementation Plan

Last updated: 2026-05-27

## Phase objective

Deliver organization intelligence modules: company mapping, OKRs, meetings, and communication preferences.

## In-scope modules

- Company tree and people search
- OKR management
- Meeting scheduling
- Email subscription preferences

## Functional scope

1. Company:
   - Company hierarchy tree
   - Manager and reporting relationships
   - People lookup with org context
2. OKR:
   - Company, team, and individual OKRs
   - Progress updates
   - Review cadence
3. Meeting:
   - Create, schedule, accept, decline, cancel
   - Agenda and attendee tracking
4. Email subscriptions:
   - Event-based notification switches
   - Role-sensitive default templates

## Role behavior

- Employee:
  - Participate in assigned OKRs and meetings.
- Manager:
  - Own team OKRs and review cycles.
- Admin:
  - Configure org-level defaults and policy links.

## Deliverables

- Hierarchy-aware company intelligence views
- OKR operations baseline
- Meeting lifecycle controls
- Notification preference center
